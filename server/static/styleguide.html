Style guide

One theme for every page: the home page, sign-in and join pages, the projects window, the GitHub page, and the editor with its panes, dialogs, Assistant and plugins. This page is drawn with that theme, static/theme.css, so what you see here is what ships. The written rules are in server/web/STYLE.md.

Principles

  • It is KiCad. Neutral grey chrome like KiCad's own windows, dense rows, small radii, 1px rules. The design on the canvas is the only colourful thing on screen.
  • Ink is the accent. The primary button is a solid ink fill; links, selection, focus and the active tab are neutral. No blue on chrome. One primary button per view.
  • Red means red. Red is F.Cu, an error, or a destructive action. It never means “go”.
  • Colour comes from tokens. No colour literal outside the theme. Canvas drawing uses KiCad's own layer and schematic colours and is the one exception.
  • Light and dark are the same design. Every surface works in both; nothing is styled for one theme only.
  • Plain words. Labels are KiCad's own where KiCad has one. Sentence case, no exclamation marks, errors say what to do next.

Type

IBM Plex Sans for everything people read, IBM Plex Mono for what the design says: references, values, coordinates, file paths, counts, keys. Weights are 400, 500 for controls and 600 for titles. Nothing is set in italics or all caps except the small mono captions.

The canvas and the mark

The board and the sheet use KiCad's own colours in both themes; the chrome around them never competes. Layer colours are KiCad's defaults and live with the renderer, not in the theme.

PCB · --canvas
silk in --silk
Schematic · --sheet

The mark is a junction: two wires and the dot that joins them, near-white on a near-black rounded tile. It sits at 18 to 20px in title bars and is the browser icon, and is never recoloured or redrawn. It is one file, static/icon.svg.
